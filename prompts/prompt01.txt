GAME CONCEPT:
A 3D floating arena of multicolored tiles. The player starts at an entry tile and must reach an exit tile by crossing tiles. There are multiple routes (shorter/riskier and longer/safer). Each round, the game announces a rule about a tile color (e.g. "Blue tiles are bouncy"). The rule is randomly TRUE or FALSE each round. The player chooses whether to trust or ignore it when picking a route. Each tile color has a real hidden effect (e.g. bounce, slide, crumble, safe). A new rule, and a new layout, appears every round. The player character is a custom 3D model (GLB) that I will provide.
